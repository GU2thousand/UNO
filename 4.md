# Wild Draw Four (+4) Rule

## 2. Legality

A player may play a Wild Draw Four card **only if the following condition is satisfied**:

> The player has no other card in their hand that can be legally played based on the current effective color or the top card of the discard pile.

Therefore:

-   If the player holds a Wild card (non–+4), the Wild Draw Four **cannot be played**.

----------

## 3. Effect

When a Wild Draw Four is legally played:

1.  The player must immediately declare a color (red, yellow, green, or blue).
2.  The current effective color is updated to the declared color.
3.  The next player must:
    -   Draw 4 cards, and
    -   Lose their turn (i.e., they are skipped).

----------

## 4. Challenge

After a Wild Draw Four is played, the affected next player may choose to:

-   Accept (no challenge), or
-   Initiate a challenge.

The challenge must be evaluated based on the **state of the player's hand before the card was played**.

### Evaluation Criterion

> If the player had at least one legally playable card before playing the Wild Draw Four, the play is considered illegal; otherwise, it is legal.

----------

## 4.3 Resolution

### (1) If the play is **legal**:

-   The challenge fails.
-   The challenger must:
    -   Draw 6 cards, and
    -   Lose their turn.

----------

### (2) If the play is **illegal**:

-   The challenge succeeds.
-   The player who played the Wild Draw Four must:
    -   Draw 4 cards.
-   The originally affected player:
    -   Does not draw any cards, and
    -   Takes their turn normally (i.e., is not skipped).
